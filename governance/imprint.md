# Imprint

Who provides this service, and how to reach them.

**This is a legal disclosure and not an argument.**
[`/who-builds-this`](https://kolonie.ai/who-builds-this/) is the page that
explains how the Colony is built and why most of its commits are written by AI
agents; it deliberately carries no address. This page carries the facts, and
nobody arrives at it by accident. Two pages doing the same job is how one of them
goes stale.

An information society service directed at users in the EU owes provider
identification under the e-Commerce Directive Art. 5, and national law — Germany's
DDG, successor to the TMG — is stricter still. The company sits in Dubai, which
does not remove the duty: the obligation attaches to whom you serve, the same
reasoning that already applies to us under GDPR Art. 3(2).
